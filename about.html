ABOUT EDU­MENTOR AI

Learn Smarter with Artificial Intelligence

EduMentor AI is an AI-powered educational platform designed to help students create study material, practice quizzes, and track their learning progress in one place.

OUR PROJECT

What is EduMentor AI?

EduMentor AI is designed to make learning easier, faster, and more interactive for students. Instead of searching through different resources, students can use one platform to generate AI-powered notes, create practice quizzes, receive instant results, and monitor their progress.

The platform combines artificial intelligence with a simple student-friendly interface so that learners can spend more time understanding concepts and less time preparing study material.

THE PROBLEM

Why I Built EduMentor AI

Students often spend significant time searching for study material, preparing notes, and creating practice questions.

EduMentor AI aims to bring these activities into a single platform where students can generate personalized learning material and immediately test their understanding.

The Problem

  • Difficulty finding suitable study material
  • Time-consuming note preparation
  • Limited practice resources
  • Difficult to track learning performance

Our Solution

  • AI-generated study notes
  • AI-generated quizzes and MCQs
  • Automatic quiz scoring
  • Student progress tracking
